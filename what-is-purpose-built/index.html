Not a conversion. Not a rehab.
Purpose built.

There's a fundamental difference between a home designed for short-term rental and one retrofitted into it. We build the first kind.

The problem with retrofits.

Walk through a typical vacation rental — one that started as a family home — and the compromises become visible within minutes. The kitchen is closed off from the living space so the group never gathers in one place. The third bedroom is a converted office with a closet-sized bathroom. The laundry is in the garage. The pool deck gets full sun all afternoon with no shade. The entry dumps guests straight into the living room with no drop zone for luggage.

None of these are design flaws in a home. But for a vacation rental that turns over weekly with new groups of 8, 10, 12 people? Every one of those choices creates friction — in the guest experience, in operations, in reviews, in returns.

Designed from the ground up for performance.

A Purpose Built property starts with a different question. Not "how do we build a nice home?" but "what does a high-performing vacation rental actually need?"

Guest experience design.

An open-plan great room connecting kitchen, dining, and living into one social space. Bedroom configurations matching real booking patterns — king primaries, bunk rooms, flexible spaces for multi-family groups. Outdoor living treated as additional square footage — covered dining, shade structures, pool layouts designed for groups.

Operational design.

Laundry located adjacent to the cleaning supply closet, not at the far end of the hall. Finishes selected for durability under 52 turnovers a year — commercial-grade flooring, quartz surfaces, performance fabrics. Access panels for fast maintenance. Smart home features for streamlined check-in and remote management.

Return-driven design.

Every square foot justified by revenue potential. Room counts matched to market demand — not builder convention. Finishes aligned with what guests search for and pay a premium to book. Energy efficiency, insurance optimization, and durability built in from day one.
