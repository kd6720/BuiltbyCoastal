A once-in-a-generation rebuild.

Fort Myers Beach is in the middle of a transformation — new infrastructure, active development, restored amenities, and proven tourism demand.

Infrastructure

The foundation is stronger than ever.

Since Hurricane Ian, Fort Myers Beach has undergone one of the most thorough infrastructure rebuilds on the Gulf Coast. These aren't plans — they're active projects with completion dates.

Estero Boulevard Repair

$9M roadway and drainage program, 25% complete, expected Fall 2027. The island's main artery rebuilt to higher standards.

San Carlos Boulevard Corridor

$13M project with smart traffic signals and seasonal transit lane — dramatically improved island access.

Water & Sewer

Fully restored and operational. Reliable utilities in place — no temporary solutions.

FEMA CRS Class 5 (2026)

25% flood insurance discount island-wide — a structural, durable cost advantage for property owners.

Storm-Hardened Town Hall

Opened September 2025 — a visible commitment to resilience and long-term recovery.

Community Connectivity

Free seasonal trolley and tram restored. Mooring field reopened (19 moorings, up to 85ft vessels). Library reopened March 2025.

Development Pipeline

$200 million says the market believes in this island.

Private capital is flowing into Fort Myers Beach at scale. Projects aren't speculative — they're open, under construction, and approved.

Margaritaville Beach Resort

$200M, 254 rooms. Opened December 2023, refinanced 2026 on strong performance.

New Fishing Pier

$17.7M investment, 70% longer than the original. Completion expected 2027-2028.

Sunset Beach Resort

$13M mixed-use at Times Square. Broke ground July 2026, opening mid-2027.

Approved Pipeline: 584 Rooms

Arches Bayfront (263 rooms), Silver Sands (+60), Pink Shell (+40). All approved, moving forward.

Scarcity. Demand. Infrastructure. Timing.

Scarcity. Barrier island. No more land being made. Buildable lots are finite and post-Ian codes mean new builds meet higher standards.

Demand. 3.27M visitors in FY24-25. Hotel rooms remain 41% below pre-Ian levels. Vacation rentals fill a genuine supply gap.

Infrastructure. Roads, water, sewer, transit, flood mitigation — all rebuilt or in active construction. The work is being done.

Timing. The rebuild is now. The pipeline is active now. FEMA Class 5 is in effect now. Investors who wait compete with the Margaritavilles for what's left.
