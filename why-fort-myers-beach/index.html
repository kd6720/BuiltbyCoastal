Infrastructure

The foundation is stronger than ever.

Since Hurricane Ian, Fort Myers Beach has undergone one of the most thorough infrastructure rebuilds on the Gulf Coast. These are active projects with completion dates — not proposals.

Estero Boulevard Repair

$9M roadway and drainage program, 25% complete, expected completion Fall 2027. The island's main artery rebuilt to higher standards.

San Carlos Boulevard Corridor

$13M project with smart traffic signals and seasonal transit lane — dramatically improved island access.

Water & Sewer Systems

Fully restored and operational. Reliable utilities in place — no temporary solutions, no uncertainty.

Storm-Hardened Town Hall

Opened September 2025 — a visible commitment to resilience and long-term recovery.

FEMA CRS Class 5 Rating

A 2026 designation delivering a 25% flood insurance discount island-wide — a durable, structural cost advantage, not a temporary incentive.

Community Connectivity

Free seasonal trolley and tram restored. Mooring field reopened (19 moorings, vessels up to 85 feet). Public library reopened March 2025.

Regional Engine

$1.7 billion in airport capacity. 25 minutes away.

Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW) is in the middle of a historic expansion — a new 14-gate Concourse E, record traffic of 11.15 million annual passengers, and a regional population growing at 15.1%.

That's not just an infrastructure project — it's a feeder system for vacation rental demand. Every new flight brings guests. Every new gate brings more. Fort Myers Beach sits 25 minutes from one of the fastest-growing airports in the country.

Concourse E Phase 1 completion target: 2027. Full expansion: 2029.

Development Pipeline

$200 million says the market believes in this island.

Private capital is flowing into Fort Myers Beach at scale. The pipeline isn't speculative — projects are open, under construction, and approved.

Open · 2023

Margaritaville Beach Resort

$200M, 254 rooms. Opened December 2023 and refinanced in 2026 on strong performance — the market validated this investment.

Under construction · 2027–28

New Fishing Pier

$17.7M rebuild, 70% longer than the original. A community landmark and tourism draw anchoring the Times Square district.

Broke ground · 2026

Sunset Beach Resort

$13M mixed-use at Times Square. Opening mid-2027 — new rooms, new retail, new energy in the heart of the beach district.

Approved pipeline: Arches Bayfront (263 rooms), Silver Sands expansion (60 rooms), Pink Shell expansion (40 rooms) — 584 hotel rooms and units approved and moving, backed by institutional capital.

Parks & Dining

A destination, not just a beach.

Forty-plus restaurants are operating — Doc Ford's, Dixie Fish Co., and The Whale's new elevated flagship among them. $11M+ in county park rebuilds are underway. The festival calendar is restored: Shrimp Festival, Sand Sculpting Championship, Island Hopper Songwriter Fest.

Investment properties don't exist in a vacuum. Guests choose Fort Myers Beach because of what's here — and what's here keeps getting better.

Why This Matters

Scarcity. Demand. Infrastructure. Timing.

Scarcity

Barrier island. No more land being made. Buildable lots are finite, and post-Ian standards mean new builds meet higher codes than anything before.

Demand

3.27M visitors in FY24–25. Hotel rooms remain 41% below pre-Ian levels. Vacation rentals fill a genuine supply gap.

Infrastructure

Roads, water, sewer, transit, flood mitigation — all rebuilt or actively under construction. The risk that plagues emerging markets doesn't apply.

Timing

The rebuild is now. Investors who wait compete with the Margaritavilles and Pink Shells for what's left.

The Vision

What's being built here goes beyond recovery.

The new Fort Myers Beach won't be the old Fort Myers Beach — it will be stronger, smarter, and better capitalized. The owners who enter during this window aren't buying into a recovery story. They're buying into what comes next.
