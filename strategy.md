## 1. Positioning Statement

Purpose Built by Coastal develops **performance-engineered vacation rental investment homes** in Fort Myers Beach, Florida — properties designed from the ground up around guest experience, operational efficiency, and return on investment, not converted from existing housing stock.

---

## 2. ICP Definitions

### ICP 1: Accredited Real Estate Investor
- **Who:** High-net-worth individuals, family offices, 1031 exchange buyers
- **Need:** Passive income through short-term rental property with predictable ROI
- **Pain point:** Can't find turnkey vacation rental properties that are actually designed for rental performance (not retrofitted homes)
- **Mapped to page:** Home, Investor Overview

### ICP 2: Institutional / Portfolio Investor
- **Who:** Small funds, REITs, hospitality groups with 3-10+ property portfolios
- **Need:** Scalable, repeatable investment properties in proven STR markets
- **Pain point:** Inconsistent build quality, operational headaches, unpredictable returns
- **Mapped to page:** Investor Overview, Contact

### ICP 3: Industry Partner / Referral Source
- **Who:** Real estate agents, wealth managers, CPAs, 1031 intermediaries
- **Need:** Reliable investment product they can recommend to clients
- **Pain point:** Most "investment properties" are just regular homes in STR zones — not purpose-built
- **Mapped to page:** About, Contact

### ICP 4: STR Operator / Property Manager
- **Who:** Vacation rental management companies seeking inventory
- **Need:** Properties that minimize turnover time and maximize guest satisfaction
- **Pain point:** Managing properties not designed for rental operations
- **Mapped to page:** Three Pillars, Contact

## 4. Messaging Hierarchy

### Primary Message
"Not a conversion. Not a rehab. Purpose built." — Every property is engineered from the ground up for short-term rental performance.

### Supporting Messages
1. **Guest Experience** — Design choices driven by what earns 5-star reviews and repeat bookings
2. **Operational Efficiency** — Layouts and systems that reduce turnover time and maintenance costs
3. **Investor ROI** — Every design decision traced back to booking data, demand patterns, and return on investment

### Proof Points
- Fort Myers Beach is a proven STR market with year-round tourism demand
- Post-Hurricane Ian rebuild means all new construction under modern building codes
- Limited barrier island land = scarcity value
- Coastal development expertise (the "by Coastal" brand equity)

### Tone & Voice
- **Confident, not salesy** — the concept sells itself; explain it clearly
- **Data-informed, not hype-driven** — reference "booking data," "demand patterns," "return on investment"
- **Premium but approachable** — this is a serious investment, but the team is accessible
- **Short, scannable paragraphs** — business buyers skim first, read second

## 5. Market Context — Fort Myers Beach Post-Ian Rebuild

*Source: Fort Myers Beach 2026 Investment Prospectus (July 2026)*

### Infrastructure — Recovery & Resilience
- **Estero Blvd repair:** $9M program, 25% complete, expected completion Fall 2027
- **San Carlos Blvd corridor:** $13M project with smart signals and seasonal transit lane — improved island accessibility
- **Water & sewer restoration:** Fully complete — reliable utilities in place
- **Storm-hardened Town Hall:** Opened September 2025 — symbol of resilience commitment
- **FEMA CRS Class 5 rating (2026):** 25% flood insurance discount island-wide — significant for property owners
- **Free seasonal trolley & tram service:** Restored, connecting the community
- **Mooring field:** Reopened with 19 moorings for vessels up to 85 feet
- **Library:** Reopened March 2025 — community resource restored

### Development Pipeline — Active & Approved Projects
- **Margaritaville Beach Resort:** $200M, 254 rooms, opened December 2023 — refinanced 2026 on strong performance, validating the market
- **New Fishing Pier:** $17.7M investment, 70% longer than original — completion 2027-2028
- **Sunset Beach Resort at Times Square:** $13M mixed-use — broke ground July 2026, opening mid-2027
- **Arches Bayfront:** 263 hotel rooms approved
- **Silver Sands Resort expansion:** 60 additional rooms approved
- **Pink Shell expansion:** 40 additional rooms approved
- **Approved lodging pipeline:** ~584 rooms/units in development — market confidence in tourism demand

### Parks & Dining — Lifestyle & Livability
- **40+ restaurants operating:** Doc Ford's, Dixie Fish Co., The Whale's new elevated flagship
- **$11M+ in county park rebuilds** underway
- **Restored festival calendar:** Shrimp Festival, Sand Sculpting Championship, Island Hopper Fest
- Active, walkable, amenity-rich destination — supports premium STR positioning

### Why This Matters for Investors
- **Infrastructure is real, not promised** — water, sewer, roads, transit are operational or in active construction with completion dates
- **Development pipeline proves market confidence** — $200M+ in private investment flowing in, not waiting
- **FEMA Class 5 = lower carrying costs** — 25% flood insurance discount is a durable cost advantage
- **Lifestyle amenities drive demand** — 40+ restaurants, festivals, parks, pier — this is a destination, not just a beach
- **Barrier island scarcity** — limited buildable land, constrained supply, proven tourism demand

## 6. Improvement Opportunities vs. Current Site

### Strengths (Keep)
- Clean, premium visual design with strong color system
- Clear three-pillar framework (memorable, scannable)
- "Not a conversion. Not a rehab." tagline is excellent
- Good page flow / intentional journey
- Inter font family is clean and professional

### Weaknesses to Address
1. **Hero section is text-heavy** — needs more visual impact, stronger imagery, less copy
2. **Social proof is missing** — no testimonials, no project photos, no partner logos, no "as featured in"
3. **Data is asserted, not shown** — "proven demand" and "booking data" are mentioned but never visualized (charts, numbers, market stats)
4. **Contact form is bare** — no qualifying fields, no clear next-step expectation setting
5. **Mobile could be tighter** — some sections run long on mobile
6. **No FAQ or objection handling** — investors have questions (minimum investment, timeline, management, exit strategy) that aren't addressed
7. **No case study or example property** — a rendered/sample property would make it tangible
8. **SEO gaps** — title tags are decent but meta descriptions, schema markup, and internal linking could be strengthened
9. **Loading performance** — Next.js on macaly.app adds overhead; a static site on GitHub Pages would be faster
10. **No blog or content section** — no way to demonstrate thought leadership or capture search traffic

### Recommended Additions
- **"Why Fort Myers Beach" data section** — concrete infrastructure stats (FEMA Class 5, $200M+ development pipeline, 40+ restaurants, festival calendar) from the 2026 Investment Prospectus
- **Development pipeline visual** — timeline or map showing active projects (Margaritaville, Fishing Pier, Sunset Beach Resort, Arches Bayfront)
- **Testimonials / trust section** — investor quotes, operator endorsements
- **FAQ section** — common investor questions answered upfront
- **Sample property render / case study** — make the concept tangible
- **"The Process" timeline** — from inquiry to keys-in-hand
- **Blog** — market updates, design philosophy, investment insights (SEO engine)
