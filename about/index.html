About Purpose Built by Coastal

Built on experience. Driven by performance.

Purpose Built by Coastal brings together vacation rental expertise, development discipline, and deep local knowledge of Fort Myers Beach — creating investment properties that work as hard as you do.

Who We Are

Developers who think like operators.

Most developers build homes. We build income-producing assets.

Purpose Built by Coastal was founded on a straightforward observation: the vacation rental industry is full of properties that were designed for living, then retrofitted for rental. The result is a constant tension between guest expectations, operational reality, and investor returns.

We saw a better way. By starting with the rental outcome and designing backward, we create properties where the guest experience feels effortless, the operations run efficiently, and the numbers work from day one. Every design decision — from the location of the laundry to the sightline from the kitchen to the pool deck configuration — is made with all three outcomes in mind.

We're not the biggest developer on Fort Myers Beach. We're the one that thinks hardest about what happens after the keys change hands.

Why Coastal

Local knowledge. Proven methodology.

Fort Myers Beach isn't just a market we entered — it's a community we know. The post-Ian rebuild isn't a headline to us; it's our daily reality. We understand the building codes, the insurance landscape, the rental demand patterns, and the infrastructure timeline because we live it.

That local knowledge is paired with a repeatable development methodology. Every Purpose Built property follows the same rigorous design framework — three pillars, data-driven decisions, construction standards built for rental durability. The result is consistency across properties and predictability for investors.
