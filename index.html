The Concept

Not a conversion. Not a rehab. Purpose built.

Most developers build homes. We build income-producing assets.

Most vacation rental properties started as something else — a family home, a beach cottage, a condo unit. They were designed for living, not for rental performance. The layout fights the operation. The finishes can't handle turnover. The guest experience was an afterthought.

Purpose Built by Coastal takes a different approach. Every property is designed from the ground up for short-term rental performance — every square foot serves the guest, every material choice accounts for operations, and every design decision connects to return on investment.

The Framework

Three pillars. One outcome.

Every Purpose Built property is engineered around three interconnected priorities — each reinforcing the other two.

01

Guest Experience

Memorable stays that earn 5-star reviews and repeat bookings through intentional design — from arrival flow to bedroom configuration to the view from the living room. Every guest touchpoint is designed, not discovered.

02

Operational Efficiency

Layouts and material choices that reduce turnover time, maintenance costs, and operational friction. The cleaning crew moves efficiently, the HVAC is accessible, and the finishes withstand 52 weeks of guests.

03

Investor ROI

Design decisions driven by booking data, demand patterns, and return on investment. We don't guess what guests want — we build what the data shows they'll pay for, season after season.

The Market

A once-in-a-generation rebuild.

Fort Myers Beach is in the middle of a transformation. Following Hurricane Ian, the barrier island is rebuilding smarter, stronger, and with higher standards than ever before.

$200 million in private development is active right now — from the Margaritaville Beach Resort to the new Fishing Pier to the Sunset Beach Resort breaking ground this summer. Over 40 restaurants are back, the festival calendar is restored, and county parks are being rebuilt.

Limited barrier island land. Proven tourism demand. Modern building codes. The conditions for vacation rental investment haven't looked this compelling in decades.
