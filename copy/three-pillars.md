## Pillar 1: Guest Experience

### Headline
Design that earns 5-star reviews.

### Body
A great guest review isn't a mystery. It's the result of a thousand small design decisions that add up to an effortless, memorable stay.

**Arrival that sets the tone.** The entry sequence matters. Guests should walk into a view, a sense of space, an immediate understanding that this property was thought through. Drop zones for luggage. Clear sightlines to the main living area. Natural light from multiple directions.

**Social space that works.** The kitchen-dining-living connection is the heart of any group rental. Open-plan design keeps the group together whether someone is cooking, eating, relaxing, or planning the day. No isolated rooms. No awkward flow.

**Bedrooms designed for groups.** King primaries with ensuite bathrooms for the couples. Bunk rooms and flexible configurations for kids and extended family. Every bedroom has natural light and thoughtful storage — no converted closets or windowless rooms.

**Outdoor living as primary space.** In Fort Myers Beach, outdoor space isn't an afterthought. Covered dining areas. Shaded lounge zones. Pool positioning that catches the right light. Landscape design that provides privacy without blocking the coastal feel.

### Design Examples
- Open-plan great room connecting kitchen, dining, and living into one continuous social space
- Primary suites positioned for privacy with dedicated outdoor access
- Bunk rooms designed for kids — durable, fun, and away from adult zones
- Covered outdoor dining with ceiling fans, lighting, and direct kitchen access
- Pool positioned for all-day sun with adjacent shade structure

## Pillar 2: Operational Efficiency

### Headline
Design that reduces the cost of every turnover.

### Body
The difference between a property that's profitable and one that's marginal often comes down to operations. Cleaning time. Maintenance access. Durability under heavy use. These aren't afterthoughts — they're design inputs.

**Turnover-optimized layout.** Laundry positioned for efficiency, not aesthetics. Cleaning supply storage integrated into the floor plan. Flooring transitions that work with commercial cleaning equipment. No awkward corners or difficult-to-reach surfaces.

**Material choices that last.** Commercial-grade luxury vinyl plank flooring that handles sand, water, and 52 turnovers a year. Quartz surfaces that resist stains and scratches. Performance upholstery fabrics that clean easily. Paint finishes that can be spot-repaired between guests.

**Maintenance built in.** Access panels for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical — not buried behind drywall. Standardized fixtures across properties so maintenance teams carry fewer spare parts. Smart home systems that allow remote monitoring of HVAC, locks, and leak sensors.

**Insurance and durability.** Elevated construction meeting post-Ian building codes. Impact-rated windows and doors. Hardened exterior materials. FEMA CRS Class 5 compliance for lower flood insurance costs. A property built to withstand coastal conditions costs less to insure and less to maintain.

## Pillar 3: Investor ROI

### Headline
Design that earns its place on the balance sheet.

### Body
Every design decision in a Purpose Built property connects to return on investment. We don't build features because they look good in a brochure. We build features because the data shows guests search for them, book because of them, and pay a premium to have them.

**Revenue-driven room configuration.** Room count and type are matched to booking platform demand data for the Fort Myers Beach market — not builder convention. We know what size groups book in which seasons, and we configure accordingly.

**Amenity ROI analysis.** Every amenity is evaluated against its impact on booking conversion and average daily rate. Pool? Yes — it's the #1 search filter in this market. Hot tub? Depends on the floor plan. Theater room? Not at the expense of a bedroom that generates revenue 365 nights a year.

**Energy and operating cost modeling.** Efficient HVAC zoning. LED lighting throughout. Smart thermostats. Tankless water heaters. Solar-ready roof design. Every efficiency gain drops straight to the bottom line — and compounds over the life of the property.

**Resilience as ROI.** Impact-rated construction. Elevated design. FEMA compliance. A property that survives storm season without damage and carries lower insurance costs delivers better long-term returns than one that looks good but can't handle the coastal environment.

### The Interconnection
Guest Experience drives reviews, which drives occupancy, which drives ROI. Operational Efficiency reduces costs, which increases margins, which improves ROI. ROI justifies the investment in design, which improves Guest Experience and Operations. The pillars aren't separate priorities — they're a closed loop.
