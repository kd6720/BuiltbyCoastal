## Section: The Problem With Retrofits

### Headline
Most vacation rentals weren't built for vacation rental.

### Body
Walk through a typical vacation rental property — one that started as a family home or a condo unit — and the compromises become visible within minutes.

The kitchen is closed off from the living space, so the group never gathers in one place. The third bedroom is a converted office with a closet-sized bathroom. The laundry is in the garage. The pool deck gets full sun all afternoon with no shade structure. The entry sequence dumps guests straight into the living room with no drop zone for luggage.

None of these are design flaws in a home. They're normal, functional choices — for a family that lives there year-round.

But for a vacation rental that turns over every three to seven days with new groups of 8, 10, 12 people? Every one of those choices creates friction. Friction in the guest experience. Friction in the cleaning and maintenance workflow. Friction in the reviews, the rebookings, and ultimately the returns.

## Section: The Purpose Built Difference

### Headline
Designed from the ground up for short-term rental performance.

### Body
A Purpose Built property starts with a different question. Not "how do we build a nice home?" but "what does a high-performing vacation rental actually need?"

The answer drives every decision that follows.

**Guest experience design.** An open-plan great room that connects kitchen, dining, and living into one social space. Bedroom configurations that match real booking patterns — king primaries, bunk rooms for kids, flexible spaces that work for multi-family groups. Outdoor living treated as additional square footage — covered dining, shade structures, pool layouts designed for groups.

**Operational design.** Laundry located adjacent to the cleaning supply closet, not at the far end of the hall. Finishes selected for durability under 52 turnovers a year — commercial-grade flooring, quartz surfaces, performance fabrics. Access panels that make maintenance fast. Lock systems and smart home features that streamline check-in and remote management.

**Return-driven design.** Every square foot justified by revenue potential. Room counts matched to market demand patterns — not builder convention. Finishes and amenities aligned with what guests in this market actually search for and pay a premium to book. Energy efficiency, insurance optimization, and durability built in from day one.

The difference is visible the moment you walk in — and measurable the moment you look at the numbers.

## Section: Why It Matters

### Headline
Better design. Better reviews. Better returns.

### Body
When a property is designed for the way guests actually use it, the experience feels effortless. Five-star reviews aren't the result of luck — they're the result of eliminating friction before the first guest ever walks through the door.

When a property is designed for the way it's cleaned and maintained, operational costs drop. Turnover time shrinks. Maintenance surprises become rare. The margins improve without raising rates.

When every design decision is connected to return on investment, the numbers work from day one — not after three years of "learning the property."

### Stat Highlights
- Higher guest satisfaction — designed experiences earn better reviews
- Lower operating costs — efficient layouts reduce turnover time and maintenance
- Stronger returns — data-driven design outperforms retrofitted properties
