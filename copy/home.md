## Section: The Concept

### Label
The Concept

### Headline
Not a conversion. Not a rehab. Purpose built.

### Body
Most vacation rental properties started as something else — a family home, a beach cottage, a condo unit. They were designed for living, not for rental performance. The layout fights the operation. The finishes can't handle turnover. The guest experience was an afterthought.

Purpose Built by Coastal takes a different approach. Every property is designed from the ground up for short-term rental performance. Every square foot serves the guest. Every material choice accounts for operations. Every design decision connects to return on investment.

The result: properties that earn better reviews, cost less to operate, and deliver stronger returns — because they were built that way from day one.

## Section: The Market

### Label
The Market

### Headline
A once-in-a-generation rebuild.

### Body
Fort Myers Beach is in the middle of a transformation. Following Hurricane Ian, the barrier island is rebuilding smarter, stronger, and with higher standards than ever before.

$200 million in private development is active right now — from the Margaritaville Beach Resort to the new Fishing Pier to the Sunset Beach Resort breaking ground this summer. The FEMA CRS Class 5 rating delivers a 25% flood insurance discount island-wide. Infrastructure rebuilds on Estero Boulevard and San Carlos Boulevard are underway with completion dates in sight. Over 40 restaurants are back, the festival calendar is restored, and county parks are being rebuilt.

Limited barrier island land. Proven tourism demand. Modern building codes. New construction that meets today's standards. The conditions for vacation rental investment haven't looked this compelling in decades.
