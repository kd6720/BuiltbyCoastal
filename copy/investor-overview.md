## Section: FAQ

### Headline
Questions investors ask.

**What is the minimum investment?**
Each property and build is unique. We discuss specific numbers during the introductory conversation — our goal is to match you with a property that fits your investment criteria.

**How long does construction take?**
Build timelines are discussed during the introductory conversation and depend on the specific property and its stage in the development pipeline. We provide regular progress updates throughout construction.

**Do I need to manage the property myself?**
No. We connect you with professional vacation rental management partners who handle bookings, guest communication, cleaning, and maintenance. You can also use your own operator if preferred. The property is designed to make professional management efficient.

**What happens during hurricane season?**
All Purpose Built properties are constructed to post-Ian building codes with elevated design, impact-rated openings, and hardened materials. Fort Myers Beach carries a FEMA CRS Class 5 rating, providing a 25% flood insurance discount island-wide. We design for resilience — and the insurance market recognizes it.

**Can I visit a completed property?**
Yes. We encourage potential investors to tour completed Purpose Built properties to experience the design quality and attention to detail firsthand. Contact us to schedule a visit.

**What kind of returns can I expect?**
Return projections are property-specific and based on Fort Myers Beach market data. We share detailed performance projections during the introductory conversation, grounded in actual booking platform data for comparable properties in the market.
